PandaProxy

A multi-service proxy for BambuLab 3D printers that solves the limited simultaneous connection problem in LAN/Development mode. Sits between the printer and your client apps — proxying the camera feed, MQTT control channel, and FTP — so tools like OctoApp, Bambuddy, and VLC can all connect at the same time without reconfiguration.
